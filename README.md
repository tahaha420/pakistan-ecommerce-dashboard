# 📦 Pakistan E-Commerce Transaction Archive (2016–2018)

A Power BI analytics project built to visualize and understand the rapid growth, payment behavior, and operational challenges of Pakistan’s online retail sector.  
Using **500,000+ real-world transactions (Mar 2016 – Aug 2018)**, this dashboard transforms raw logs into actionable business intelligence.

👉 **retail_dashboard.pbix**  


---

## 🚀 Project Overview

This dashboard was developed for **E-commerce Managers, Stakeholders, and Market Analysts** to:

- Monitor revenue growth  
- Detect anomalies and operational issues  
- Analyze customer behavior and payment trends  
- Support data-driven decision making

The project demonstrates strong expertise in **data cleaning, ETL, DAX, and visual storytelling** inside Power BI.

---

## 🛠️ Skills & Techniques Showcased

### ⚙️ Advanced ETL & Data Cleaning
- Fixed inconsistent date formats  
- Standardized columns  
- Removed/handled null values  
- Cleaned categorical fields for accurate grouping  

### 🧮 DAX Measures
Custom measures created for key business KPIs:
- `Total Revenue`
- `Total Orders`
- `Customer Count`
- `Average Order Value (AOV)`
- Additional time-intelligence measures

### 📉 Time Series Analysis
- Continuous line charts  
- Year/Month hierarchy drill-down  
- Identification of seasonality and peaks (major spike in early 2018)

### 📊 Composition & Behavioral Analysis
- Donut charts for payment methods  
- Bar charts for top-selling categories  
- Category-level revenue contribution (Mobiles & Tablets ≈ **50% of total sales**)

### 📐 Grid-Based UI/UX Layout
- Clean card-style KPI design  
- Balanced spacing, shadows, and modern dashboard layout  
- Portfolio-ready visual architecture

## 📊 Dashboard Breakdown

### **1. Executive KPI Panel**
A high-level overview of business health:
- **Total Revenue:** ~Rs 5 billion  
- **Total Orders:** 585K  
- **Customer Base:** 115K  
- **AOV:** Rs 8.5K  

### **2. Sales & Operational Trends**
- Yearly and monthly revenue trajectory  
- Visual identification of growth patterns  
- Highlighted revenue boom in early 2018  

### **3. Category & Payment Insights**
- **Top Category:** Mobiles & Tablets (≈50% revenue share)  
- **Payment Method Mix:** COD dominating over digital channels (JazzWallet, Easypay, etc.)

---

## 📝 Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Power BI can transform a messy, large-scale transaction log into an insightful analytics command center.  
By structuring, cleaning, and visualizing half a million transactions, the dashboard reveals:

- Strong revenue growth  
- Customer payment behavior  
- High reliance on COD  
- Key category drivers  
- Opportunities for improving digital payment adoption
